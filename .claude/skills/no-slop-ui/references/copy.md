# Copy

Words are interface. Every line has one job.

- **Say what it is and who it's for** in the hero, in plain words. "Student-built tech for a smarter TIS." beats "Innovating the future of education".
- **Sentence case**, active voice, plain verbs, no filler, no exclamation marks.
- **CTAs name the action** and keep the same verb through the flow: button "Share idea" → toast "Idea shared" → email "We got your idea".
- **Specific beats clever**: real names, real numbers, real places ("Testing in B-block", "157 delegates").
- **Errors** say what happened and how to fix it, without apologising: "Add your email so we can tell you if it's picked."
- **Empty states** invite the next action: "No ideas yet. Be the first — it takes one sentence."
- **Labels** above fields, never placeholder-as-label. Hints say why you're asking.
- **Length**: hero heading ≤ 8 words, lead ≤ 25 words, section heading ≤ 6 words.
- **Multilingual sites** (EN/RU/UZ): write each language natively, don't machine-mirror sentence structure; check line breaks per language.

Replace on sight: unlock, elevate, seamless, supercharge, empower, revolutionise, next-gen, cutting-edge, world-class, game-changing, effortless, leverage, harness, "Welcome to", "Get started today", "Learn more", "Submit", "Click here".
