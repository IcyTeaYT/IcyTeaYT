# Shot recipes for product films

Replace `{PRODUCT}` with the product bible paragraph, verbatim. Each recipe = start-frame image prompt (IMG) + motion prompt (VID).

## 1. Factory / studio establish
IMG: Wide 24mm shot of a clean modern {workshop type} with rows of machines, soft overhead fluorescent light, {PRODUCT} in the foreground slightly out of focus, shallow depth of field, photoreal commercial photography, neutral grade.
VID: Slow low dolly forward toward the product, background machines softly defocus. Subtle dust in the light. Product stays identical.

## 2. Material macro
IMG: Extreme macro 100mm of the {material} of {PRODUCT}, individual fibres/grain visible, raking side light, crisp texture, dark background.
VID: Very slow lateral slide across the surface, focus breathes slightly. No other changes.

## 3. Process (stitch / pour / cut / press)
IMG: Macro of a {tool: sewing needle / nozzle / blade} mid-action on {PRODUCT part}, thread/liquid/material visible, hard rim light, black background.
VID: The {tool} moves in a realistic repeating rhythm, {thread tightens / liquid flows / material separates}, camera locked off, slight focus pull at the end.

## 4. Logo / label reveal
IMG: Macro 85mm of the logo on {PRODUCT}, text perfectly legible, soft key light from top-left, gentle falloff.
VID: Static camera, light slowly sweeps across the embroidery/print left to right creating a highlight. Text must remain unchanged and legible.

## 5. Hero on pedestal
IMG: {PRODUCT} centred on a glossy dark pedestal, dramatic single top light, subtle reflection, deep navy/black background with faint haze, premium ad look.
VID: Slow 45° orbit around the product at eye level, reflections move naturally, haze drifts. Logo stays legible.

## 6. Colour-backdrop hero (social/clean)
IMG: {PRODUCT} floating against a saturated {brand colour} seamless backdrop, soft shadow below, studio strobe light, crisp edges.
VID: Product rotates 20° and settles, gentle bob, shadow follows.

## 7. Lifestyle wear/use
IMG: Candid 35mm photo of a {person description, age, style} {wearing/using} {PRODUCT} on a {city street / café / gym}, golden-hour backlight, natural skin, film grain.
VID: Handheld tracking shot walking alongside, subject glances toward camera, natural motion blur, lens flare as sun passes behind a building.

## 8. Golden-hour flare close-up (ending)
IMG: Close-up of {PRODUCT} with the sun flaring through behind it, warm haze, bokeh, logo readable.
VID: Slow push-in, flare blooms and softens. End frame holds 1 second.

## 9. Splash / elemental (drinks, cosmetics, food)
IMG: {PRODUCT} mid-splash of {liquid}, droplets frozen, backlit, black background, high-speed photography look.
VID: Super slow motion, liquid arcs around the product and falls away, droplets catch light.

## 10. Exploded view (tech/hardware)
IMG: Exploded technical view of {PRODUCT} with components floating in layered order, clean studio light, grey background.
VID: Components slowly assemble into the finished product, camera eases in, final click into place.

## 11. Unboxing (UGC feel)
IMG: Top-down phone-camera shot of hands opening a {packaging} containing {PRODUCT} on a wooden table, natural window light.
VID: Hands lift the lid and pick up the product, slight handheld movement, realistic fingers.

## Fixes for common failures
- Logo warps → shorter clip, slower/static camera, regenerate start frame with clearer logo, or composite the real logo in post.
- Product changes colour → repeat exact colour words, add "same colour as start frame", attach reference image again.
- Extra fingers / weird hands → frame hands smaller or out of focus; pick the best take.
- Plastic "AI look" → add film grain, "shot on 35mm", imperfect natural light; grade all shots together in the edit.
