# Redesign option B: Tashkent Metro line

Scope: /redesign/metro, a full redesign option shown beside the live site. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: TIS students (mostly on phones), teachers, parents. Job: be impressed and trust that these students build real things (TISMUN is the proof), then send an idea.
User decisions: very premium, NO LAG above all; avoid hacker-y and anything close to the current site; dolly camera moves are the motion grammar. Content and facts unchanged.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The council is a metro line from idea to launch; every section is a station the camera rides into. Refuses the scrolling stack of interchangeable sections.
OWN-WORLD: Station marble #ECE8E0 and polished granite #16181B, brass #B08D57 for signage rules, Kosmonavtlar ceramic blue #1D4F91 for vaults; the four logo petals are line colours only. Big Shoulders Display caps for station signs (English with Uzbek beneath), Onest for text. Rounded vault arches, brass rules, line-map dots.
STORY: Platform at the first station with the line map > ride the tunnel > Mission station > About station > TISMUN station with the departure-board countdown > Founders station > Ideas station (send an idea) > Questions > end of the line footer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Granite platform wall, the line map across the top as navigation, station sign "TIS TECH COUNCIL" with the headline beneath on a marble panel, the tunnel mouth glowing at right, "Suggest an idea" as the platform button.
FORM: Tashkent metro line, position 1 of 7 (impeccable's pick), seed key f3360738. Signature: the tunnel dolly between stations (transform-only rings) and the line map that tracks the train.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
