# Redesign option A: Tashkent Modernism mosaic

Scope: /redesign/mosaic, a full redesign option shown beside the live site. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: TIS students (mostly on phones), teachers, parents. Job: be impressed and trust that these students build real things (TISMUN is the proof), then send an idea.
User decisions: very premium, NO LAG above all; avoid hacker-y and anything close to the current black/white Inter site; dolly camera moves are the motion grammar (depth chosen by us). Content and facts unchanged.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The council as a 1970s Tashkent modernist building whose science mosaic is made of student ideas, one tile each. Refuses the dark tech landing with neon.
OWN-WORLD: Warm concrete #CFC9BE ground, ink #1C1A17, four smalt tile colours from the logo petals (#F29839, #951E34, #07686E, #0897B6) and a white-smalt ground tile; nothing else, no greys, no gradients. Unbounded (wide, uppercase, building-sign lettering) over Onest text. Square-cornered concrete panels, lattice (panjara) openings, hairline grout lines.
STORY: Facade with the headline as a building sign > dolly through one lattice opening into the mosaic hall > mission line on the mural > what we do in the lattice cells > TISMUN plaque with a building-clock countdown > founders in concrete niches > send an idea, it becomes a tile > questions > foundation stone footer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Concrete facade filling the frame, a grid of arched openings with the mosaic glimpsed through them; the logo as emblem top left; headline in wide caps on the facade band, lower third; ink plaque with the line and "Suggest an idea".
FORM: Tashkent modernist mosaic, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key f3360738. Signature: one continuous dolly (approach, pass through the lattice, glide the mural, push into a tile). Raises: Ikeda total palette commitment; zoo-map single geometry (lattice grid frames sections and drives nav); night-flight damped motion; Ray Gun one full-bleed monumental mission line.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
